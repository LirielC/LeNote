LeNote is a handwriting-first editorial notebook for Android tablets and phones, crafted with a custom "Warm Paper & Editorial Ink" design system and powered by Google's Jetpack Ink engine (androidx.ink).

Features:
• Low-Latency Stylus Writing: Pressure-sensitive pen with 7 curated inks (including White Ink for dark/OLED sheets), highlighter, and stylus barrel button eraser toggle.
• Analytical Partial Stroke Eraser: Slices and trims vector strokes at the exact capsule intersection in real time.
• Lasso Selection & Calligraphy Refinement: Freeform polygon selection to move, recolor, duplicate, beautify handwriting, or snap strokes to paper rulings.
• 9 Paper Templates & 4 Paper Stocks: Blank, Ruled, Grid, Dotted, Mathematics, Cornell Notes, Weekly Planner, Engineering, and Music Staff across White, Legal Yellow, Soft Sepia, and OLED Graphite paper tones.
• Hierarchical Organization: Organize thoughts into Notebooks, Sections, and Sheets, with Favorites, Recent Sheets, and Soft-Delete Trash.
• Local-First & Portable: Full vector PDF import & annotation, vector PDF export, high-resolution PNG export, and .lenote backup packages.
• Multi-Language Support: Automatic Android system locale detection and manual language selector supporting English, Portuguese, and Spanish.
